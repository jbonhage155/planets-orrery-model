# 8. Assembly Procedure

This is the order in which the orrery is built. It follows the project's build checklist (eleven phases, 0 to 10) and the column build sheet. All sizes are in inches unless marked mm. Each step says who does it: **Kid**, **Grown-up** or **Together**. Steps already completed are marked *(done, date)*; the rest are still to do.

The box was redesigned on 11 Nov 2026 (lower ceiling, one sloped front panel, ceiling that slides out the back). Phases 7 and 8 below follow the new design. Where the printed checklist page still shows the earlier box, this procedure takes precedence.

## 8.1 General rules and safety

- Safety glasses for everyone nearby when cutting, drilling or cutting spring-steel wire. Gloves for epoxy.
- The Dremel is loud and throws hot chips: children watch from a step back.
- Foam balls melt under super glue (CA), acrylic cement and spray paint. Use only brush-on acrylic paint, epoxy or white glue on the foam.
- Leave the protective film on acrylic and polycarbonate until each panel is finished.
- Label every tube and panel with masking tape and a marker.
- Light oil between tubes, never grease.
- Never dimple, pin or hard-clamp a brass tube: it would pinch the tube inside it.

## 8.2 Phase 0: Get ready

1. **Lay out every part and check it against the parts list** (Together). Acrylic (two thicknesses), polycarbonate, brass tubes, 1/8 in stainless rods, 16 gears, nylon washers, 1/8 in collars, M5 threaded rods and nuts, thumb nuts, magnets, rubber feet, 3/32 in music wire, foam balls, paints, cement, epoxy, light oil. *(done, 24 Oct 2026)*
2. **Set up the work table.** Scrap wood to drill onto, masking tape and marker, steel ruler and square, a clamp, safety glasses, gloves.

## 8.4 Phase 2: Cut the tubes

Tools: mini tube cutter, tapered reamer. Measure every length from the tube's factory end. Work from the fattest, shortest tube inward.

| Order | Part | Tube | Cut to (in) | mm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Neptune (practice cut) | 13/32 | 2 5/16 | 58 |
| 2 | Uranus | 3/8 | 3 7/16 | 87 |
| 3 | Saturn | 11/32 | 4 9/16 | 117 |
| 4 | Jupiter | 5/16 | 5¾ | 146 |
| 5 | Mars | 9/32 | 6⅞ | 175 |
| 6 | Earth | 1/4 | 8 1/16 | 204 |
| 7 | Venus | 7/32 | 9 3/16 | 233 |
| 8 | Mercury (cut last) | 3/16 | 10⅛ | 257 |
| – | Sun post | 1/8 stainless rod | uncut, 16 | 406 |

1. **Mark each length** (Together). Check the factory end is square. Wrap masking tape with its edge on the mark for a square line all the way round.
2. **Cut Neptune first** as a practice cut (Grown-up). Cutter wheel on the tape edge, press lightly, tighten about ⅛ turn per revolution. The 0.014 in wall crushes easily.
3. **Cut the other seven**, Uranus to Mercury (Grown-up).
4. **Ream every cut end** (Grown-up). The cutter rolls a lip inward. Ream until the next smaller tube slides through under its own weight, then sand the outside edge lightly.
5. **Check and label each tube** (Kid). Length within ±1/32 in (±1 mm); stands without rocking; turns freely on the next tube along its whole length.
6. **Leave the Sun post uncut** at 16 in (Together). The extra goes up inside the Sun. Trim only if longer than 16¼ in. The 1/16, 3/32, 1/8 and 5/32 in tubes are spares; no sleeve is used.

*(Phase 2 done by 7 Nov 2026.)*

## 8.5 Phase 3: Gears onto tubes, and the washers

Tools: 29-piece drill set, tapered reamer, 5-minute epoxy, calipers.

| Planet | Drill its gear | Gear's bottom face above the base (as built) | Washer under the tube |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mercury | 3/16 | flush with the tube end, 2 3/16 | none: sits on the collar |
| Venus | 7/32 | 2 11/16 | M5, as it comes |
| Earth | 1/4 | 3 3/16 | M5, drilled to 7/32 |
| Mars | 9/32 | 3 11/16 | M6, as it comes (snug) |
| Jupiter | 5/16 | 4 3/16 | M6, drilled to 9/32 |
| Saturn | 11/32 | 4 11/16 | M6, drilled to 5/16 |
| Uranus | 3/8 | 5 3/16 | M8, drilled to 11/32 |
| Neptune | 13/32 | about 5 23/32 (gear top 5⅞) | M8, drilled to 3/8, plus about ¼–⅜ in of extra nylon washers |

1. **Drill 8 gears**, one to each tube's size (Grown-up). Clamp the gear flat, drill slowly with the exact-size bit, ease a tight hole with the reamer from both faces. Aim for a snug slide, not a press fit. Keep the other 8 gears for phase 4.
2. **Mark where each gear sits** (Kid). Mercury's gear is flush with its tube end. Every other gear is set in the stack with calipers, **8.5 mm** from the top of the gear below to its own bottom face (Neptune 12.5 mm, to clear Mars's gear screws). The 40-tooth gears are about 4 mm thick, so the gear levels come out **½ in apart**.
3. **Epoxy each gear on** (Grown-up). Roughen the tube under the gear. Epoxy inside the hole and its moulded slots only, never on the gear's faces. Let it cure fully.
4. **Make the 8 washers** from the nylon kit (Grown-up). "Drill" means open the hole to that size, with the washer clamped between two scraps of wood.
5. **Dry-fit all eight** on a spare steel rod, washers between (Together). Every tube must turn freely without dragging its neighbour.

*(Phase 3 done, 7 Nov 2026. Designed level spacing was 0.65 in for 8 mm gears; as built it is ½ in.)*

## 8.6 Phase 4: Servo gears and the first mesh test

1. **Bolt a 40-tooth gear to each servo's metal disc** (Grown-up): 4 × M3 screws with washers through the gear's own holes. There is about ±0.9 mm of play; centre it by eye before tightening. Keep each servo with its planet label (1 Mercury … 8 Neptune).
2. **Mesh one servo gear with one tube gear** at **1.575 in (40.0 mm)** between centres (Together). Turn one full revolution by hand. Feel for tight spots and watch for wobble.
3. **Direction check** (Together): run that servo from the touchscreen. The planet must turn anticlockwise seen from above. If it turns clockwise, change the software setting ARM_DIR to −1. (to check: result not recorded)

## 8.7 Phase 5: Base and centre column

1. **Practise** one cut and one drilled hole on scrap (Grown-up).
2. **Base:** the ¼ in acrylic sheet as it comes, 12 × 12. Choose the back edge. Mark the Sun post hole **6 in from either side and 3¾ in from the back edge** (behind the middle, so the outer planets swing less far over the screen) (Together).
3. **Drill the post hole**: 1/8 in, 3/16 in deep, **not through** (Grown-up). Tape on the bit 3/16 in from its tip marks the depth.
4. **Epoxy the Sun post in**, standing upright (Together). Check with a square from two sides at right angles while the epoxy sets.
5. **Fit the shaft collar**: slide the 1/8 in collar down the rod and tighten its set screw with its top **2 3/16 in** above the base (Grown-up).
6. **Stack the planets** (Together). Mercury's tube straight over the rod onto the collar, no washer; its gear's bottom face is then 2 3/16 in above the base. About 1/32 in of play on the rod is fine; if it rattles, wrap a few layers of masking tape round the rod just above the collar and near Mercury's top. Then Venus to Neptune in order: the washer onto the gear of the tube inside, then the tube. A drop of light oil between tubes.
7. **Neptune's height**: about ¼–⅜ in of nylon washers go under Neptune's tube, on Uranus's gear, so Neptune's tube top is about **8¼ in** above the base and its arm clears the ceiling. Raise Neptune's servo stand to match.
8. **Check** every tube turns freely and does not drag its neighbour (Kid). To raise or lower the whole stack, loosen the collar's set screw and slide it.

*(Phase 5 done, 7–8 Nov 2026; Neptune's tube top measured 8¼ in on 8 Nov.)*

## 8.8 Phase 6: Servos on their stands

Servo order round the column, seen from above, going anticlockwise from the front: **Mercury** (front), Jupiter, empty (right), Mars, Neptune, Earth (back), Uranus, Venus (left), Saturn. Servo shafts are 1.575 in from the column. Each stand is a ⅛ in acrylic plate on two M5 rods under the servo's outer end only (a full-length post would hit the next servo's gear).

| Servo | Plate top above base (as built) | Two rods, each (as cut) |
|---|---|---|
| Mercury | 0.67 | 35 mm |
| Venus | 1.17 | 51 mm |
| Earth | 1.67 | 68 mm |
| Mars | 2.17 | 84 mm |
| Jupiter | 2.67 | 101 mm |
| Saturn | 3.17 | 117 mm |
| Uranus | 3.67 | 134 mm |
| Neptune | about 4.19 | 150 mm |

The rods were cut for the 0.65 in design; with ½ in levels the plates sit lower, and the spare rod above each nut does no harm. The thumb nuts set the real height, with a ruler across both gears.

1. **Cut 8 stand plates**, 2 × 1⅞ in, from ⅛ in acrylic (Grown-up). Score a 4 11/16 in strip off the back-door sheet, snap two 2 in strips off it, and cut each into 1⅞ in pieces (12 plates: 8 + 4 spare). Drill two 13/64 in rod holes **1⅜ in apart**, 0.42 in in from the far-end edge.
2. **Fix each servo to its plate** (Grown-up). Plug its cable(s) in first: the plugs lie flat under the servo. Set the servo centred side to side, its far end 5/32 in in from the plate's far edge. Dot its four bottom holes through the clear plate and drill 7/64 in. Stack M2 nylon washers on each screw to fill the 1/16 in gap. Four M2 × 10 self-tapping screws up through the plate, snug, not hard.
3. **Cut the 16 M5 rods** with a hacksaw from seven 250 mm rods: 150 + 84 (×2), 134 + 101 (×2), 117 + 117, 68 + 68 + 51 + 51, and 35 + 35 (Grown-up). Run a nut on before cutting; unscrewing it cleans up the thread.
4. **Mark the rod holes on the base from the real gears** (Together). Hold each servo at its height with its gear meshed against its tube's gear, and mark the two rod holes under its outer end.
5. **Drill the 16 rod holes**, 13/64 in, through the base (Grown-up).
6. **Fit the stands and set each height** (Together). Each rod goes through the base, a nut on top and a washer and nut underneath. Thumb nut under the plate (one turn = 0.8 mm), a lock nut on top. Ease the rods apart about 1/32 in to drop the plate on.
7. **Chain the cables**, 8½ in each: driver board → Mercury → Venus → Earth → Mars → Jupiter → Saturn → Uranus → Neptune. Each runs along its plate's side edge, outside the lock nut, off the plate's outer end and round the outside of the ring, slack bowed away from the gears, with a dab of tape on each plate.
8. **Fit four rubber feet** under the corners of the base (Grown-up). A 1/8 in hole at each corner, 0.9 in in from both edges. Each foot on an M3 × 16 mm screw, head down in the foot's recess with a nylon washer under the head, a nut on top, snug not squashed. *(done, 25 Oct 2026)*
9. **Run every servo on its own** from the settings screen in MANUAL (Together). Mercury first: watch its gear mesh through a full turn for tight spots, wobble or skipping, set the mesh and height, then the next. Check the three tight spots found in the 3D model: Mercury's, Venus's and Earth's servo-gear screws pass 0.05 in from the stand plate four slots up (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus). If one touches, snap a corner off that plate or drop the screw washers.
10. **Check the settings screen** shows every servo connected.

*(Phase 6 done: servos mounted 7 Nov 2026; column, gears and servos in and running by 9 Nov 2026.)*

## 8.9 Phase 7: Measure the screen, then build the box

Materials: ⅛ in acrylic (sides, back door), ⅛ in polycarbonate (front panel, ceiling), the ¼ in base already in place. Every long cut is made with a Dremel 3100, its 565 cutting guide and the 561 bit along a clamped straightedge. Nothing is snapped (a 12 in score-and-snap ran off the line). Full-size paper templates for the sides, ceiling and back door are printed from the panel templates page at 100 %.

| Panel | Material | Size | Holes and notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sides ×2 | ⅛ acrylic | 12 deep × 6¾ tall | Front corner cut along the slope: from 3/32 in back at the floor to 5 3/16 in back along the top edge. Left side: 20 mm switch hole, centre 1.55 in up from the bottom of the box (1.3 in up the panel's own edge), 4.0 in from the front; 8 mm charging socket hole, centre 1.45 in up (1.2 in up the panel), 1.0 in from the back. (to check: hole positions carried over from the taller box). Right side: 1/8 in converter standoff holes, marked from the converters |
| Front panel | ⅛ polycarbonate | 8⅜ × 12, one piece | Screen opening about 6 11/16 × 4 1/16 (the tray size), cut first. Stands on the base, outside face flush with the base's front edge, leans back 37.1° from upright until its top corner meets the ceiling. Covers the sides' sloped edges and is glued to them; no separate front wall |
| Ceiling | ⅛ polycarbonate | 12 wide × 6 15/16 deep | Not glued. Rests on the sides' top edges and the front panel's top corner. A ¾ in slot from its back edge to a ¾ in hole round the column (3¾ in from the back, 6 in from the side). Slides out the back |
| Back door | ⅛ acrylic | 11 11/16 × 6 23/32 | Lift-off, not glued. Sits under the ceiling. 1 in finger hole, centred side to side near the top edge. Four 8 × 3 mm magnets |
| Magnet blocks ×4 | ¼ acrylic offcut (or two ⅛ scraps glued) | 0.75 × 0.75 | In the four inside corners of the back opening, tops below 6¾ in |

*Replaced (11 Nov 2026): a 7.85 in tall box with a separate 3.5 in front wall, a 5.0 in sloped strip at 35°, a lid resting on blocks with a 13/32 in bearing hole for the column, and a collar under the lid on Neptune's tube.*

1. **Measure the screen** (Together): glass 7⅝ × 4⅜; metal tray behind it about 6⅝ × 4.0; lip 9/16 at the left, 7/16 at the right, ¼ at the top, ⅛ at the bottom (seen from the front); lit picture 6 1/16 × 3⅜. *(done, 9 Nov 2026)*
2. **Practise with the Dremel on scrap and measure the guide offset** (Grown-up). Acrylic scrap first, then one short cut near a corner of the spare polycarbonate sheet. Dial about 4 to 6 (15,000–20,000 rpm). Measure from the straightedge to the near edge of the slot; that offset is typed into the template page so it shows where the straightedge goes for every cut.
3. **Cut the front panel's screen opening** while the sheet is whole (Grown-up). Stick the printed template into a corner of the sheet. Drill a ¼ in hole inside each corner, then make the four cuts hole to hole, in order. Cut a touch small and file until the tray just drops in (only ⅛ in of lip at the bottom). Then cut the panel to 8⅜ × 12. *(opening cut by 11 Nov 2026)*
4. **Cut the two sides** (Grown-up). Cut one, check it against the base and the column, then trace it onto the second. Drill their holes while they lie flat: switch hole with a step bit to ¾ in, then file to 20 mm; socket hole with a step bit to 5/16 in, then file to 8 mm.
5. **Tape the sides to the base** and measure the gap between them (Together). The back door is cut to the measured gap.
6. **Cut the ceiling and its slot** (Grown-up). Measure where the column passes the ceiling's height (tube centre = near edge + 13/64 in) before printing the ceiling template.
7. **Cut the back door** to the opening and drill its 1 in finger hole with a step bit (Grown-up).
8. **Dry-fit the whole box** with masking tape, the screen set in its opening (Together). Check every tube still turns freely and the ceiling slides in and out round the column.
9. **Test the glue on offcuts**: acrylic to acrylic, and polycarbonate to acrylic. Acrylic cement may not hold polycarbonate; if the test joint peels, use 5-minute epoxy for the front panel.
10. **Glue the walls** (Grown-up): sides onto the base, flush with its edges, taped square; then the front panel onto the sides' sloped edges. Outdoors or at an open window. Hold each joint and run thin cement in from the needle applicator. Parts that need holes in the base near the front (driver board) go in first.
11. **Back door magnets** (Together). Cement the four blocks into the inside corners of the back opening, their back faces **0.36 in** in from the back edge so the door lands flush. Epoxy (not cement) a magnet on each block and one on the door's inside face opposite each. Test that each pair attracts before gluing.

## 8.10 Phase 8: Electronics into the box

The electronics were wired and tested on the battery on 3–4 Oct 2026, outside the box. Everything goes in through the open top, because the screen and the front of the box cannot be reached from the back door past the servo ring.

1. **Set up the DROK converter** before any servo is connected (Grown-up): input only, switch on, show OUT volts, hold ON/OFF until it shows ON, turn the CV trimmer to 7.5 V, check with a multimeter. Optional: set the CC current limit to about 3 A. Switch off, then connect OUT+/OUT− to the driver board. *(done, 3–4 Oct 2026)*
2. **Switch and charging socket** into the left wall (Grown-up).
3. **Battery** on its edge against the left wall, 12 V jack at the top, LEDs facing in; velcro to the base starting 1.4 in from the back edge (Grown-up).
4. **Converters** on the right wall: 7.5 V converter centred 2.8 in behind the middle, 0.3 in off the floor, on 0.1 in standoffs; 5 V converter just in front of the middle. **Driver board** on the floor, front left. Driver board jumper on the USB side (Grown-up).
5. **Screen into the front panel from the front**, stuck by its own tape (Grown-up). The Pi stays screwed down on the screen's back plate. Fit the stick-on heatsinks on the Pi. Route the Pi clear of the Jupiter-to-Saturn servo cable.
6. **Tie down every cable** so a bump cannot pull it out (Grown-up), especially the screen ribbon, the servo power leads and the chain cable.
7. **Switch on** (Together): the screen starts, the settings screen shows every servo connected, and there is no under-voltage warning.
8. **Slide the ceiling in** from the back, its slot round the column (Together). It is not glued. No collar goes under it (a collar on top of Mercury's tube stops the stack floating up, and each arm's collar holds the tube below down).
9. **Fit the back door** on its magnets.

The builder confirmed with the real screen and Pi that nothing touches a servo (11 Nov 2026).

## 8.11 Phase 9: Arms, planets and the Sun

| Planet | Out from the column centre (in) |
|---|---|
| Mercury | 3½ |
| Venus | 5 7/16 |
| Earth | 7½ |
| Mars | 9½ |
| Jupiter | 12 3/16 |
| Saturn | 15 7/16 |
| Uranus | 18 5/16 |
| Neptune | 21 |

The planets are evenly spaced, about 1 1/16 in of empty space between neighbouring balls. Neptune sweeps a circle about 43½ in across: about 18 in behind the box, 16 in past each side and 13½ in in front. Rod lengths and arm heights are in mechanical-design.md (4.6 Arms).

1. **Cut the arm rods**: straight 3/32 in music wire, no bends (Grown-up). Hard spring steel: bolt cutter and safety glasses (the cut end flies). *(done, 8 Nov 2026)*
2. **Epoxy each rod into its collar**, flat on the bench, the rod's far end propped level. 24 hours before a planet hangs on it (Grown-up). *(done, 8 Nov 2026)*
3. **Put the arms in travel mode** from the settings screen: every servo turns so its arm will point away from the screen.
4. **Clamp each collar on its tube**, pointing straight away from the screen (Together). After the ceiling is in. Clamp screw only, no glue on the tubes; one wrap of electrical tape under the three loose collars (Mars, Saturn, Neptune). Uranus's collar overhangs its tube top by about ⅛ in, so its arm hole is drilled below the collar's middle. (to check: clamping in progress 14 Nov 2026)
5. **Fit the elbow and upright** at each arm's tip: an aquarium elbow, the rod roughened and glued in one leg and a short upright in the other, with epoxy or gel super glue (keep super glue away from foam). Bend the long outer arms slightly up before gluing (they droop about ¾–1 in at Neptune). Check each arm clears the ones above and below all the way round.
6. **Push each planet onto its upright** with epoxy or white glue, never CA (Kid). All planets end level, about 14.3 in above the base.
7. **Glue Saturn's ring on, tilted 25°** (Together), about the direction Saturn travels: one edge tips up toward the Sun, the other dips on the outside, level front to back. Turn Saturn by hand past Jupiter and Uranus before the glue sets: about ¾ in to spare each side.
8. **Fit the Sun** (Kid, with help). Make a straight pilot hole first: twist a skewer in from a dot on the bottom toward a dot exactly opposite on top, about 3½ in deep. Push the Sun down over the post until its bottom is about ⅛ in above Mercury's tube, check it sits level with the planets, then glue at the bottom with epoxy or white glue. Too high: trim the rod with heavy wire cutters.

## 8.12 Phase 10: Calibrate and test

1. **Check the line** (Together): in travel mode every planet should stand in one straight row pointing away from the screen. For any planet off the line: settings → MANUAL, tick that planet, step it with the 1° or 10° buttons onto the line, then SAVE HOME (two taps). RESET HOME forgets a saved home. *Replaced (13 Nov 2026): parking each servo at step 0 and recording an offset per planet.*
2. **Press Today** and check a few planets against a sky app or NASA's Eyes on the Solar System (Kid).
3. **Run at several speeds**; listen for clicks and jerks, watch for any arm catching (Together).
4. **With the box closed, watch the settings screen for 20 minutes** (Grown-up): Pi temperature, and that it never shows "throttled".
5. **Battery run time**: charge full, run the planets, note the LED steps (Grown-up).
6. **Practise the explanation** for the science fair (Kid).
