Panel templates: side, ceiling, back

The lower box (11 Nov 2026): the ceiling at 6¾ in, one sloped front panel from the floor to the ceiling, a ceiling that slides out the back. Full-size paper templates for the two sides, the ceiling and the back door. All sizes in inches; heights are from the floor of the box (the top of the base).

1. The new shape

  • Front: the 8⅜ × 12 polycarbonate piece with the screen opening, screen end up. It stands on the base, its outside face flush with the base's front edge, and leans back until its top corner meets the ceiling. It is 12 in wide, so it covers the sides' sloped front edges and is glued to them; no separate front wall.
  • Sides: ⅛ in acrylic, as tall as the ceiling's underside, the front corner cut to the panel's slope.
  • Ceiling: ⅛ in polycarbonate, 12 in wide (no width cut). It lies on the side walls' top edges and the front panel's top corner, not glued. A ¾ in slot from its back edge goes round the column, so it slides out the back without touching the arms, and the tubes needn't be exactly upright. It is no longer the column's top bearing.
  • Back door: lift-off as before, on its magnets, now sitting under the ceiling: the ceiling slides out over its top edge.
  • The collar under the lid on Neptune's tube is dropped (the collars above hold the tubes down).

2. Numbers

Fractions like 6 3/4 can be typed as they are. The sides and the ceiling can be printed now. Print the back door after the sides are taped to the base: type in the gap you measure. Measure the column where it passes the ceiling's height (to the tube's centre: its near edge plus 13/64) before printing the ceiling.

3. How to use them

  1. Print portrait at 100 % ("actual size", not "fit to page"). Check the 2 in bar on every page with a ruler.
  2. Trim the paper along the lines marked ✂ and stick it on the sheet, film still on, each trimmed edge flush on a factory edge. Glue stick, or tape all round. Cut straight through the paper.
  3. Side and ceiling: the two pages each go in their own corner of the sheet and overlap in the middle. The long cut runs across both: check the red line runs straight on from one page to the other.
  4. Back door: part 1 in the bottom left corner; part 2's trimmed edge on part 1's MATCH LINE.
  5. Check with a ruler before cutting. Each page prints the distances to check.
Red = cut line: the edge of the piece you keep. The pink band is the ⅛ in bit, on the waste side. Blue = the straightedge's edge (shaded side = the straightedge), arrow = push this way. Holes: prick the centre through the paper, then drill.
