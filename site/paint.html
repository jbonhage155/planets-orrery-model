Paint the Solar System

Each card shows what the planet really looks like (from NASA pictures), which paint bottles to use, and how to paint it. No mixing: every colour comes straight from the bottle. Tap I painted it! when you finish one.

Before you start

  1. Push each ball onto a skewer so you can hold it. Stand it in a cup of rice to dry.
  2. No mixing! Every colour goes on straight from the bottle. Squeeze a little onto the palette.
  3. Paint two thin coats of the main colour. Wait about 20 minutes between coats. Thin is better than thick!
  4. When the main colour is dry, add the details: stripes, spots, clouds.
  5. Wash your brush in water before you change colour.
  6. Only use the paint. Super glue and spray paint melt the foam balls!

Planet pictures: Solar System Scope, based on NASA data (CC BY 4.0). Paint names are from the Caliart 24-colour set; if a bottle's name is a little different, pick the one that looks closest.
